# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the PhantomOS community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We commit to an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on technical collaboration, not personal attacks.
- Respect boundaries and privacy.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive language.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and related communication channels.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports or private information in a public issue.

For reports that require confidentiality, use the repository's private reporting channel documented in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the report as a **Code of Conduct** matter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate corrective action when violations occur.
